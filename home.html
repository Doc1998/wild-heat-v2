Sauna Design

A great home sauna begins with a design that genuinely fits your space — not a template dropped into your bathroom or garden. We build every custom sauna ourselves — no subcontractors, no cut corners. We build indoor saunas (converting spare rooms, utility spaces, or basement areas), standalone outdoor cabins, barrel saunas, and integrated wellness rooms with separate steam and cold plunge areas. Questions & Answers

Everything You Want to Know About Home Saunas

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often. Still not sure? Just call us.

How much does a home sauna cost in Ireland?

Home sauna costs in Ireland vary widely depending on size, materials, and complexity. A compact indoor cabin sauna typically starts from around €4,000–€7,000 fully installed. A mid-range custom build with premium timber and integrated controls usually falls in the €8,000–€15,000 range. Larger bespoke outdoor saunas or full steam rooms can range from €15,000 upwards. Our pre-built barrel saunas start from €4,400. We provide fully itemised free quotes, so you know exactly what you are getting before any work begins.

Do I need planning permission for a home sauna in Ireland?

In most cases, a residential sauna — particularly an indoor installation or a modest outdoor structure — falls within normal exempted development under Irish planning regulations. However, this depends on the size of the structure, its proximity to boundaries, and your local authority's specific rules. We advise all clients on this during the design consultation and, where required, can guide you through the appropriate process.

What types of sauna do you build?

We design and build a full range of home saunas: traditional Finnish dry saunas, infrared saunas, barrel saunas, outdoor cabin saunas, and full steam rooms. We also offer a range of pre-built barrel saunas ready to order. Every custom sauna is bespoke — designed around your space, your lifestyle, and the finishes you love. We work with premium cedar, thermowood, and other natural timbers suited to the Irish climate.

How long does it take to build a home sauna?

Timelines depend on the scope of work. A straightforward indoor sauna installation typically takes between three and seven days on site. A larger custom outdoor structure can take one to three weeks. Pre-built barrel saunas are typically the fastest option, since the unit itself is ready to order and simply needs to be delivered and sited. We will give you a clear project timeline at the design stage, and our team keeps disruption to your home to an absolute minimum throughout.

Do you install saunas outside of Galway?

Yes. While we are based in Galway, Wild Heat Saunas works across all of Ireland — Leinster, Munster, Connacht, and Ulster. We have installed home saunas from Cork to Donegal, Dublin to Clifden. Travel costs may apply for certain areas; we will be transparent about this in your quote.

Are your saunas fully custom, or do you sell pre-made kits?

We offer both. Our custom saunas are designed and constructed specifically for you — tailored to your dimensions, your timber preference, your heating system, and your aesthetic vision. We also stock a range of quality pre-built barrel saunas in five sizes, in pinewood or thermowood, for clients who want a faster turnaround at a lower price point.

What heating systems do you use?

We install a range of sauna heating systems to suit different preferences and budgets, including traditional wood-burning kiuas, electric sauna heaters, and infrared panels. Our pre-built barrel saunas come with an electric Harvia 9kW stove as standard, with a wood-burning stove upgrade available. We work with market-leading heater brands known for reliability and efficiency, and will advise on the most suitable option for your chosen sauna type and usage habits during the design consultation.

What timber do you use for your saunas?

We primarily work with Western Red Cedar — prized for its natural thermal resistance, low resin content, and beautiful warm tone that deepens with age. We also use thermowood (heat-treated timber), Nordic spruce, and aspen depending on the application and client preference. Our pre-built barrel saunas are available in pinewood or thermowood. All timber is responsibly sourced. We will walk you through the options at your consultation so you can choose what suits your space and taste.

Is Wild Heat Saunas fully insured?

Yes. Wild Heat Saunas carries full public liability insurance and all of our installation work is carried out to the highest professional standards. We are happy to provide proof of insurance on request before any work commences.

What is the process from first contact to completed sauna?

The journey starts with a free consultation — either on-site in Galway and surrounds, or by video for clients further afield. We then produce a bespoke design and fully itemised quotation, or point you to the right pre-built model. Once you are happy to proceed, we schedule your build or delivery. Our team handles everything: groundwork (if required), framing, insulation, cladding, heater installation, and finishing. We hand over a completed, tested sauna ready for its first session — and stay available after handover if you have questions.

What are the health benefits of a home sauna?

Regular sauna use is widely associated with a range of wellbeing benefits, including improved cardiovascular circulation, muscle recovery after exercise, stress relief, better sleep quality, and skin health. Having a sauna at home means you can build a consistent wellness habit without the need to travel to a gym or spa. We always recommend consulting your GP if you have any underlying health conditions.
